<!-- 5 · research -->

What the research says

  • Notes then one summary beats a single running summary: running summaries keep adding instead of condensing, and long summaries neglect the middle. BooookScore, NAACL 2025
  • Reading in order with a carried message beat RAG and full-context models by up to 10%. Chain of Agents
  • Page-range trees (title, first/last page, summary) are the standard vectorless index. PageIndex
  • Page-level chunks were the most consistent in NVIDIA's benchmark. NVIDIA
  • Keep visuals next to text: table screenshots are cheap insurance; captions help answers more than search. UniDoc-Bench, ColPali
  • Web pages: print "pages" are arbitrary, so a summary plus heading outline, not a page index. llms.txt

8 research angles, 80 claims fact-checked: 39 confirmed, 40 confirmed with corrections, 1 wrong and dropped.

Your decisions

Nothing is built until you approve it in your own words. Tick as you decide (saved in this browser only).

Smaller decisions
  • Turn on Docling's heading-level stage after a free comparison on the samples
  • Hidden or invisible text: leave it out of the .md (it stays in docling.json), or keep it
  • Later outputs, each yes or no: chunks.jsonl, page images, index.json, content-hash cache, Docling's local chart model
Risks
  • The model may skip an optional note; the gap is logged, never repaired.
  • A wrong heading level can carry forward; the page image always wins.
  • Summary and index accuracy can't be checked automatically; they are labelled AI navigation aids and tested with known facts.
  • Hidden instructions in documents; the agents have no tools except submit.
  • Documents over 300 pages in one final call are untested.
