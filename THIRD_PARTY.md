## Docker and local models

- Python 3.13.9 and Debian Bookworm retain their component-specific licences.
- [LibreOffice](https://www.libreoffice.org/licenses/) is distributed under
  MPL-2.0 and includes other components. Its Debian copyright/licence files
  remain in `/usr/share/doc/libreoffice*/`. Keep those notices and the relevant
  source availability obligations if distributing a built image.
- [Chromium](https://www.chromium.org/chromium-os/licenses/) has BSD-style
  project licensing and many third-party licences. Playwright downloads the
  browser; retain the browser's included licences rather than treating it as MIT.
- Font packages keep their Debian copyright notices: DejaVu/Bitstream,
  Liberation, Carlito and Caladea have their own terms, including font licences.
- CPU PyTorch and its native libraries have mixed Apache/BSD/MIT/other
  permissive notices; retain the wheel's licence bundle.
- Docling weights are downloaded to a runtime cache, not committed or embedded
  by this Dockerfile. The configured models observed in that cache are
  [docling-layout-heron](https://huggingface.co/docling-project/docling-layout-heron)
  (Apache-2.0),
  [TableFormer in docling-models](https://huggingface.co/docling-project/docling-models)
  (CDLA-Permissive-2.0 / Apache-2.0 as marked by the model card), and
  [CodeFormulaV2](https://huggingface.co/docling-project/CodeFormulaV2)
  (CDLA-Permissive-2.0). Model licensing is separate from Docling's MIT code.
  RapidOCR/Paddle model assets likewise retain upstream Apache notices.

Calling LibreOffice/Chromium and loading these models does not make their files
MIT. This task prepares source publication, not Docker-image redistribution.
Review upstream licences/source offers before publishing a binary image.

## Samples and community policy

Ten inputs are generated by the original scripts in `samples/`; names,
addresses and measurements in those documents are fictional.
The Wikipedia Markdown article output is an adaptation by everymd of work by
[Wikipedia contributors](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Markdown&action=history),
licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
That output, including its derived images/JSON, remains under CC BY-SA 4.0;
see [samples/README.md](samples/README.md) for source and adaptation date.

The README demo ([docs/assets/](docs/assets/), source in
[videos/readme-demo/](videos/readme-demo/)) also shows page images from the
[Statistical Abstract of the United States 1920, part 3](https://www.census.gov/library/publications/1921/compendia/statab/43ed.html)
(U.S. Census Bureau) and a figure from [NIST IR 8425](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/ir/8425/final), both works of the United States Government and in the public domain in the
United States; their descriptions are everymd's own output. The demo is rendered
with [HyperFrames](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes) (Apache-2.0), which
is not a dependency of everymd.

[CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) adapts Contributor Covenant 2.1 by
